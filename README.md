# SQL_PIZZA_SALES_ANALYSIS
PROJECT OVERVIEW
Hello, here are my SQL queries for analyzing pizza sales data to uncover key business insights.
This project involves analyzing a pizza sales dataset using MySQL to uncover key business insights and answer critical questions related to sales performance, customer preferences, and operational efficiency.
**Technologies Used**
Database Management System: MySQL

**Key Business Questions Answered**

We addressed several business questions to help optimize sales and operations:
What is the total revenue generated from pizza sales?
What are the top 3 most ordered pizza types based on revenue?
How does product size affect sales and customer choice?
What is the distribution of orders by hour of the day?
What is the percentage contribution of each pizza category to total revenue?
Key Insights
Analyzed revenue trends over time, highlighting peak sales periods.
Identified the most popular and profitable pizza categories and sizes.
Tracked cumulative revenue growth, providing visibility into overall business performance.

**How to Explore**
Clone this repository to your local machine.
Import the provided CSV files into your MySQL environment.
Execute the SQL queries provided in the sql_quest_*.sql files to view the analysis and insights.
